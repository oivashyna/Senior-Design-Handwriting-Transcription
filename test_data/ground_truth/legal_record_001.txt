In the matter of the estate of William Ashford, deceased.
Inventory taken the 14th day of November, in the year 1742.
Item: one oak writing desk, valued at 3 shillings and 6 pence.
Item: twelve leather-bound volumes, law and philosophy.
Item: silver candlesticks, a pair, valued at 1 pound 4 shillings.
Witnessed by James Cornwell, Notary Public, City of Bristol.
The above inventory was certified correct by the executor.
